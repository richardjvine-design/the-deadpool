A parlour game. Nobody is dying on a schedule. The point is to make each other laugh, predict cleverly, and crown a champion.

The Currency

Every member begins with 1,000 Reaper Coins. Fictitious. They exist to keep score.

The Rankings

Every player ranks every candidate and predicts a departure year. The group average determines the official death order.

The Three Wagers

  • Rank · 3× — Bet on final position.
  • Date · 5× — Bet on departure year.
  • Cause · 10× — Bet on manner. Group votes.

Settlement

Bets settle at season's end. Closest forecaster takes the pot.

Conduct

Keep causes funny, not cruel. If someone passes, retire their entry with respect.
